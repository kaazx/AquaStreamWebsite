About Us

Who We Are

We are a group of students who set out to solve a problem we saw in our own neighborhoods. AquaStream was built by a team passionate about using technology to support the small businesses that keep our communities running.

What We Offer

A web-based order tracking and management system built for water refilling station owners. With one simple dashboard, owners can:

  • Log new orders in seconds
  • Track pending and completed orders in real time
  • Manage order history
  • View daily and weekly sales
  • Sign up and log in securely

The Purpose

To give small and medium-sized water refilling stations the same operational clarity as larger businesses, so owners spend less time managing chaos and more time growing.

Our Story

We kept waiting on water orders that got lost in notebooks. The problem was not effort, but tools. So we built a free, simple system to log orders, track deliveries, and monitor sales, one order at a time.
